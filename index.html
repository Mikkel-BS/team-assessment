Methodology

What this assessment measures

The model synthesizes constructs repeatedly associated with effective teamwork. It intentionally avoids claiming that this exact 28-item questionnaire has been separately validated.

Psychological safety

Interpersonal risk-taking, speaking up, asking for help, and surfacing mistakes.

Edmondson (1999)

Shared direction & mental models

Clarity about goals, priorities, roles, and how work fits together.

Hackman/Wageman; Salas et al.

Communication & coordination

Timely information exchange, confirmation, and synchronization of interdependent work.

Hoegl & Gemuenden; Salas et al.

Mutual monitoring & accountability

Awareness of progress, constructive challenge, and reliable follow-through.

Salas et al.

Backup & mutual support

Helping overloaded colleagues and reallocating effort when demands shift.

Salas et al.; Hoegl & Gemuenden

Adaptability & learning

Adjusting plans, learning from outcomes, and changing routines when evidence warrants it.

Salas et al.; Edmondson

Trust & team orientation

Prioritizing collective success, assuming constructive intent, and using diverse contributions.

Salas et al.; Hoegl & Gemuenden

Enabling conditions & leadership

Useful structure, access to resources, coaching, and a context that supports effective teamwork.

Team Diagnostic Survey

Scoring and interpretation

Each item uses a 1–5 agreement scale. Dimension scores are means of their three items. The overall functioning index is the unweighted mean of the eight functioning dimensions; the four outcome indicators are shown separately and are not included in that index.

Labels such as “developing” or “strong” are descriptive cut-points for reflection, not population norms. For team aggregates, the app also calculates the standard deviation across respondents for each dimension to make divergent experiences visible.

Best practice: discuss patterns, low dimensions, and high disagreement together. Averages can conceal subgroups or uneven experiences.

Privacy model

Designed so the website itself cannot collect your responses

  • Responses live only in JavaScript memory and disappear on refresh/close.
  • No cookies, localStorage, IndexedDB, analytics, trackers, or service worker.
  • A strict Content Security Policy blocks network connections from the app.
  • Export happens only when you click an export button.
  • LLM analysis is a generated text prompt. The site does not transmit it anywhere.

If you paste the generated prompt into an external LLM, that provider’s privacy and data-retention terms apply.
